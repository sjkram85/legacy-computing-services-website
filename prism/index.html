Frequently Asked Questions

Do we have to install software on a special Windows laptop?

No. Prism runs 100% in any modern web browser — Windows, Mac, Chromebook, iPad, or phone. Multiple directors can work on the same tournament simultaneously without passing USB sticks around.

What happens if the bowling alley Wi-Fi drops mid-squad?

Prism includes built-in Offline Score Queue protection. If Wi-Fi flickers, entered scores are safely saved on your device and automatically sync to the server the second your connection returns — or you can tether to a phone hotspot anytime.

Can other associations or bowling centers see our bowlers or finances?

Never. Every organization operates inside its own strictly isolated workspace. Your Master Bowler Directory, tournaments, payouts, staff accounts, and historical archives are 100% private to your organization.

How do Prism A.I. credits work — will we ever get a surprise bill?

Never. Manual scoring, standings, brackets, kiosk TVs, PDFs, and exports are always 100% unlimited. A.I. credits only count when you use Flyer Import, Floor Photo OCR, or the Rulebook Assistant, and your Admin dashboard shows live usage at all times.

Does Prism support USBC WinLABS and Youth SMART scholarships?

Yes. Prism generates USBC SMART scholarship CSV files, WinLABS Excel & PDF packets, Certified Final Results PDFs, and masks bowler USBC IDs automatically on public Lobby Kiosk TVs and Bowler Portals.

How fast can we launch our association or bowling center?

Same day. Once you’re ready, we provision your organization workspace, configure your bowling centers and lane counts, issue your Admin login and Staff Join Code, and include an 8-page Director SOP PDF plus narrated video walkthrough right inside the app.
