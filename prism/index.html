Frequently Asked Questions

Does Prism integrate with Brunswick Sync, QubicaAMF BES-X, or Steltronic?

Brunswick and QubicaAMF keep their scoring networks closed and charge steep fees (plus front-desk IT setup) for proprietary connections — which is the exact reason Prism built Phone TV Photo OCR. Instead of needing a scoring-vendor API, Prism works with every scoring system on earth (Brunswick Sync, Vector, Frameworx, QubicaAMF BES-X, Conqueror, Steltronic, even 25-year-old CRT monitors). A floor helper simply snaps a photo of the overhead monitor with their phone and Prism reads the scores into the desk in ~1 second — zero cables, zero IT firewall changes, and zero vendor API fees. Prism also imports Excel/CSV rosters and exports directly to USBC WinLABS & SMART.

Do bowlers have to pay a monthly subscription to check their league averages on mobile?

Never — Prism’s "My Leagues" Bowler Dashboard is 100% free forever ($0/month, zero ads). Bowlers who bowl multiple nights a week simply type their name, email, or USBC ID once (or tap the personalized link in their morning-of-league lane reminder email) and Prism automatically tracks all their Monday–Sunday leagues, composite average, tonight’s lanes & opponent, and game-by-game logs — installable straight to their iPhone or Android home screen. On league night, every printed Standing Sheet also includes a QR code for the Prism Bowler Terminal, letting teams either write on paper recap sheets or scan-to-submit scores (typed or Photo OCR) straight to the League Secretary.

Do we have to install software on a special Windows laptop?

No. Prism runs 100% in any modern web browser — Windows, Mac, Chromebook, iPad, or phone — and also includes a 1-click Installable App (PWA) so your staff can pin Prism right to their desktop taskbar or iPhone/iPad home screen without App Store approvals or IT admin installs. Multiple directors can work on the same tournament simultaneously.

What happens if the bowling alley Wi-Fi drops mid-squad?

Prism includes built-in Offline Score Queue protection. If Wi-Fi flickers, entered scores are safely saved on your device and automatically sync to the server the second your connection returns — or you can tether to a phone hotspot anytime.

Can other associations or bowling centers see our bowlers or finances?

Never. Every organization operates inside its own strictly isolated workspace. Your Master Bowler Directory, tournaments, payouts, staff accounts, and historical archives are 100% private to your organization.

Why is Prism an annual flat-rate plan instead of a one-time download or per-bowler fee?

Traditional Windows bowling programs charge upfront plus yearly support renewals while locking you to one PC, and many newer platforms charge $1–$3+ per bowler — which quickly costs active associations $1,000+ a year out of their prize funds. Prism uses one predictable flat rate with $0 per bowler that covers 24/7 cloud hosting, live Lobby Kiosk TVs, Bowler Portals, automated backups, branded emails, ongoing updates, and monthly Prism A.I. credits.
