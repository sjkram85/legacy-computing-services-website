Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026 · Bel Air, Maryland

This page describes what this website actually does with information. Legacy Computing Services LLC does not sell personal information.

Contact form

When you submit the contact form, the site sends a copy of what you entered to our company Google Workspace. A Google Apps Script adds that copy to a Google Sheet so we can respond. The copy includes your name, email address, the service you selected, any timeline or budget note, and the description you wrote.

The same form also prepares a message in your own email app, addressed to scott@legacycomputingservices.com. That email is not sent until you press Send in your email app. This website does not keep its own database of inquiries, and the page cannot confirm that Google stored the sheet copy.

Chat assistant

The homepage chat assistant sends the message you type, along with the recent messages in that chat, to Google’s Gemini API through a Google Apps Script proxy. The proxy asks Gemini for a reply and returns that reply to your browser. The proxy does not save the conversation. The messages stay in the browser tab until you refresh or leave the page.

Please do not type sensitive personal information into the chat. That includes passwords, payment card numbers, government ID numbers, and health details.

The site stores a small note in your browser so the chat suggestion does not keep appearing after you open or dismiss it. That note is not an advertising or analytics cookie.

Employee portal

The employee portal is for our staff. Staff sign in with Google. It is not a customer account. Company files opened there stay in Google, under the account that signed in.

Hosting, fonts, and cookies

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Pages also load Tailwind CSS and Google Fonts from their content networks. Those services may log standard request data, such as an IP address and the file that was requested.

This site does not use advertising cookies or analytics cookies.

Contact

Questions about this page, or a request to correct or delete an inquiry we have, can go to scott@legacycomputingservices.com.
